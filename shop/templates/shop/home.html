The Craft & Heritage

Why Coffee Lovers Choose Us

We obsess over every variable: water TDS levels, burr alignment, roast profiling, and temperature curves.

🌍

Ethical Direct Trade

We work directly with smallholder coffee farmers, paying up to 140% above fair-trade minimums to support regenerative agriculture.

🔥

Micro-Batch Roasting

Roasted weekly in small 12kg batches on our vintage cast-iron drum roaster to capture delicate peak aroma and terroir.

🥛

Artisan Plant Milks

Partnering with oat, almond, and whole-milk creameries formulated specifically for barista micro-foam and latte art.

Zero Wait Pickup
